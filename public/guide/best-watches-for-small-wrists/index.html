How to judge fit

Diameter is only part of it. Lug-to-lug length, the distance from the tip of one lug to the other, decides whether a case overhangs the sides of your wrist, and the watch pages state it for only some of these models. Where it isn’t stated, compare the case size and thickness with a watch you already wear. Then check the strap or bracelet: a bracelet may need links removed, and a strap sized for a larger wrist may not tighten enough. A round and a rectangular case of similar width wear differently, so don’t compare them on one number.

The Citizen Tsuyosa is a common search on this topic. Its stated case is 40 mm, so it isn’t on this list; see its guide to check its other dimensions yourself.
