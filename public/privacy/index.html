Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 helps us understand visits and clicks on Amazon links only after you explicitly select Analytics and save your choices. Before that analytics choice, or after you withdraw it, we do not configure Google Analytics or send analytics events. The separate advertising choice controls Google Ads. You can use the site and follow links without accepting. Analytics is limited to dailywatchfinds.com; development and preview sites do not send it.

With consent, we send the page’s canonical path, a fixed page title and approved campaign labels. For an Amazon click we also send the catalog model, link placement and whether the destination is a product listing or model search. We remove URL queries and fragments, omit referrer URLs and do not send search-box text, names, email addresses or arbitrary campaign text. A click is not an order, checkout, commission or qualifying sale.

Google may process technical information received with the request, including IP address and browser information, and use first-party analytics cookies with a pseudonymous browser identifier to measure visits. This is not anonymous data. Our tag sets a 30-day cookie lifetime without extending it on each hit. We do not enable Google Signals, advertising personalization, enhanced conversions or account linking. Advertising measurement requires its separate choice. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

Change or withdraw your choice

Use Measurement choices in the footer to accept or reject analytics at any time. Rejecting Analytics stops future analytics measurement from this page and clears this site’s accessible Google Analytics cookies. Requests already sent cannot be recalled. After a tag has loaded, changes to either choice refresh the page to apply the new permissions. We honor browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by keeping analytics off.

For each purpose, we store only your accepted or rejected preference and its date in this browser’s local storage for up to 180 days. It is not sent as an analytics event. If browser storage is blocked or cannot be updated, a choice may not persist and measurement stays off. If saving a choice fails, we also try to store a first-party refusal cookie for that purpose for up to 180 days. It contains only a refusal flag, never an identifier. A later explicit acceptance clears that refusal. Clearing browser storage resets the choice.

Optional advertising measurement

Advertising measurement is a separate, initially off choice. Accepting analytics alone does not enable it. If you choose advertising measurement, the Google Ads tag uses advertising cookies, browser local storage and approved Google ad-click identifiers (gclid, gbraid or wbraid) to measure whether an ad visit leads to a click on an Amazon link. We do not send an order, purchase amount, customer list or checkout details. This is an observation-only outbound-click action, not a purchase conversion or proof of a qualifying sale.

After that opt-in, the Google tag may contact Google and DoubleClick services to initialize measurement and record page visits, even before you click an Amazon link. These Ads requests are separate from Google Analytics and can occur while Analytics is off. Google receives technical request information and pseudonymous cookie or ad-click identifiers for measurement. Ad personalization remains denied. This integration does not add remarketing audiences, Google Signals or enhanced conversions. Ads measurement is enabled only on dailywatchfinds.com after that separate choice. Analytics can remain off while advertising measurement is on, or the reverse.

Use Measurement choices to change either purpose. Withdrawing a purpose saves the choice, discards pending events and clears this site’s accessible cookies for that purpose, plus Google Ads linker local storage (_gcl_ls) when advertising is withdrawn. The page reloads to unload the running Google tag; any still-accepted purpose can start on the refreshed page. The existing tag may send a cookieless consent-status request during this change. Requests already sent or in flight cannot be recalled. We store each preference and its date separately for up to 180 days; existing analytics permission never grants advertising permission. Browser privacy signals keep both purposes off.

Search and filtering

There are no user accounts, email signups or shopping carts here. Search and filters run in your browser; we do not store or send those choices.

Hosting and images

Vercel may process technical request information to deliver and secure this site. Manufacturer image services receive the requests needed to serve product images. These requests support the site independently of optional analytics.

Affiliate links and other websites

Amazon links contain our affiliate tag. Following a link takes you to Amazon, whose privacy and tracking practices apply there. Manufacturer source links also lead to other websites with their own policies. We do not receive your Amazon checkout details through this site.
