Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 helps us understand visits and clicks on Amazon links only after you choose Accept analytics. Before that choice, or after you reject, we do not load the Google tag or send analytics events. You can use the site and follow links without accepting. Analytics is limited to dailywatchfinds.com; development and preview sites do not send it.

With consent, we send the page’s canonical path, a fixed page title and approved campaign labels. For an Amazon click we also send the catalog model, link placement and whether the destination is a product listing or model search. We remove URL queries and fragments, omit referrer URLs and do not send search-box text, names, email addresses or arbitrary campaign text. A click is not an order, checkout, commission or qualifying sale.

Google may process technical information received with the request, including IP address and browser information, and use first-party analytics cookies with a pseudonymous browser identifier to measure visits. This is not anonymous data. Our tag sets a 30-day cookie lifetime without extending it on each hit. We do not enable Google Signals, advertising personalization, user-provided data or cross-product linking. Advertising consent stays denied. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

Change or withdraw your choice

Use Analytics choices in the footer to accept or reject analytics at any time. Rejecting stops future measurement from this page and clears this site’s accessible Google Analytics cookies. Requests already sent cannot be recalled. If you accept again after withdrawing, your new choice takes effect on your next page visit. We honor browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by keeping analytics off.

We store only your accepted or rejected preference and its date in this browser’s local storage for up to 180 days. It is not sent as an analytics event. If browser storage is blocked, a choice may not persist and analytics may remain off. Clearing browser storage resets the choice.

Advertising measurement

Google Ads measurement is disabled. We do not configure an Ads destination or send Ads conversion events. Your analytics choice does not grant advertising permission.

Search and filtering

There are no user accounts, email signups or shopping carts here. Search and filters run in your browser; we do not store or send those choices.

Hosting and images

Vercel may process technical request information to deliver and secure this site. Manufacturer image services receive the requests needed to serve product images. These requests support the site independently of optional analytics.

Affiliate links and other websites

Amazon links contain our affiliate tag. Following a link takes you to Amazon, whose privacy and tracking practices apply there. Manufacturer source links also lead to other websites with their own policies. We do not receive your Amazon checkout details through this site.
