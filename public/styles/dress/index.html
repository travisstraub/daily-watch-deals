Dress watches

A dress watch can be round, rectangular or oval. Choose a shape and size that suit the clothes and jewelry you already wear.

How to choose

Compare the rectangular Seiko SWR085 with the oval Timex Cavatina. For a round case, look at the sapphire-crystal SUR633; for solar charging and decorative detail, the SUT068. If it’s a gift, check the size the wearer prefers before choosing.
