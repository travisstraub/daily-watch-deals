Compare the space on your wrist

The AE-1200 is 6.9 mm wider and 4 mm thicker; with its bracelet it weighs 70 g more. Those differences are useful starting points for fit, not a guarantee of comfort. Compare the width, length and thickness with a watch you already wear; a bracelet may also need sizing.

Which tradeoff would you notice?

  • For a modest case: start with the F-91W’s smaller dimensions. Try a similarly sized watch before ordering if bulk or sleeve clearance is your main concern.
  • For a recurring second time zone: the AE-1200 gives that task a dedicated function. If you only need local time, consider whether you want its extra modes.
  • For timed routines: choose the AE-1200 for countdowns and multiple alarms. The F-91W covers an elapsed-time stopwatch and one daily alarm.
  • For the silver look: the AE-1200’s bracelet is steel; its case is resin. Do not mistake its finish for an all-metal case or substitute the lighter black resin-band AE-1200 variant.

For water activities, follow Casio’s usage guidance rather than treating a depth rating as a universal permission. The F-91W’s basic rating and the AE-1200’s 100 m rating are different; neither comparison is a test of real-world durability.
