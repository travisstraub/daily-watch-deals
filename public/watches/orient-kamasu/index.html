Why consider it

Orient took the Kamasu's teeth-like hands and markers from the barracuda it is named after. Underneath, it pairs a sapphire crystal and 200 m water resistance with the in-house F6922 automatic, which hacks, hand-winds and shows the day and date.

The tradeoff

At about 41.8 mm with a 22 mm bracelet it wears like a full-size diver, so check the fit if you have smaller wrists.
