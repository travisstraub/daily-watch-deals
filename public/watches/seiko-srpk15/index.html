Why consider it

Seiko built this Cocktail Time around a brilliant blue gradated dial with a pressed pattern. A box-shaped Hardlex crystal and a see-through caseback frame the 4R35 automatic, which hand-winds and keeps a power reserve of about 41 hours.

The tradeoff

Hardlex is Seiko's hardened mineral glass, not sapphire, and the 50 m rating suits everyday wear rather than swimming. Seiko's own store listed it as sold out when checked.

THE ESSENTIALS

Know what you’re choosing.

  • 40.5 mm stainless steel case, 11.8 mm thick, 47.5 mm long
  • Caliber 4R35 automatic, about 41-hour power reserve
  • Box-shaped Hardlex crystal, see-through caseback
  • 50 m water resistance, 20 mm brown leather strap
