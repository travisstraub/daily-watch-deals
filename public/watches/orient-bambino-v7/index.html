Why consider it

Orient describes this Bambino as everything the line is known for in a more compact 38mm form. The domed mineral crystal gives the white dial its depth, and the in-house F6724 automatic hacks and hand-winds.

The tradeoff

Water resistance is 30 m, so treat it as a dress watch rather than one for swimming. The mineral crystal is less scratch resistant than sapphire.

THE ESSENTIALS

Know what you’re choosing.

  • 38.4 mm stainless steel case
  • Domed mineral crystal
  • In-house F6724 automatic, hand-winding and hacking, 40-hour power reserve
  • 30 m water resistance, 20 mm leather strap
