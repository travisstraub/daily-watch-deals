Why consider it

Casio sells the W-217H-1AV as an easy reader: large time digits on a 43.1 x 41.2 mm resin case that still weighs only 32 g. It adds an LED backlight, a daily alarm and 50 m water resistance.

The tradeoff

It is noticeably larger than an F-91W, so check the size if you prefer a small digital. The glass is resin.
