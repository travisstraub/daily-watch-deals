Why consider it

A silver dial, dark subdials and a domed crystal give the Marlin a vintage-inspired look. The quartz chronograph offers stopwatch functionality without the winding routine of a mechanical movement.

The tradeoff

The acrylic crystal is part of the retro appeal, but it needs more care around scratches than sapphire. Check the model number: Marlin variants use different straps and movements.
