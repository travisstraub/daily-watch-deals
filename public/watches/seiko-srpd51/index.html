Why consider it

The SRPD51 brings together a blue sunray dial, rotating bezel and day/date display. Its automatic movement and exhibition caseback make it an appealing introduction to mechanical watches with a sporty appearance.

The tradeoff

At 13.4 mm thick, it deserves a fit check under a cuff. Expect to reset it after time unworn: the stated power reserve is approximately 41 hours.
