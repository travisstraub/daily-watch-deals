Why consider it

Casio calls the EFR-574DE-7AV a classic Edifice chronograph in a design that is all about sports: a blue ion-plated tachymeter bezel, a day and date display and a solid steel bracelet with a three-fold clasp, rated to 100 m.

The tradeoff

At 148 g it is heavy for a quartz watch, and the crystal is mineral rather than sapphire.
