Why consider it

This 36 mm field watch combines a traditional appearance with solar charging and sapphire. It is a useful alternative to a compact automatic if you like field styling but want less winding and resetting.

The tradeoff

The leather strap is not the best choice for repeated soaking. Solar operation still needs light, so follow the charging guidance if you rotate it with other watches.
