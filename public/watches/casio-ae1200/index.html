Why consider it

World time, five alarms and a countdown timer give this digital watch a useful travel toolkit. Choose the steel-bracelet version if you like the silver look and want more wrist presence than the F-91W.

The tradeoff

The bracelet is steel, but the case and glass are resin. Its busy display and multiple modes suit someone willing to learn the controls.
