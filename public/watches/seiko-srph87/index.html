Why consider it

Choose the SRPH87 for a clean blue dial and date window, without a rotating bezel or chronograph layout. The exhibition back gives you a view of the mechanical movement.

The tradeoff

Its approximately 41-hour reserve means a weekend off the wrist can leave it needing a reset. At 41.2 mm, it's also larger than our compact field and dress selections.
