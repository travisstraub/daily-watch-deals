Why consider it

A slim resin case and digital display make the F-91W a practical starting point for someone who wants time, an alarm and a stopwatch without a charging routine. At 21 grams, it also makes sense if you dislike a heavy watch.

The tradeoff

The basic water-resistance rating is a reason to look elsewhere for swimming. Its resin glass also calls for care around scratches.
