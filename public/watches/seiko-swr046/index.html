Why consider it

A 15.5 mm case width gives this two-tone rectangular watch a distinct jewelry-like scale. It makes sense for someone who wants the watch to complement a bracelet rather than dominate the wrist.

The tradeoff

The very small black dial may not suit someone who needs larger numerals. Its 30 m water rating is another reason to choose a different watch for water activities.
