Why consider it

A black dial with 12- and 24-hour markings pairs naturally with the green fabric strap. The exhibition back adds a mechanical detail for someone who wants more interaction than a basic quartz field watch.

The tradeoff

The domed acrylic crystal can scratch, and the strap has leather backing. It is better approached as an everyday field-style watch than as maintenance-free equipment.
