Why consider it

The Promaster Diver is rated for scuba diving at 200 m and runs on Citizen's E168 Eco-Drive movement, which charges from any light. The blue dial uses luminous hands and markers, and the crystal is anti-reflective mineral.

The tradeoff

At 44 mm it is the largest watch in this group, so it suits bigger wrists best. The crystal is mineral rather than sapphire.

THE ESSENTIALS

Know what you’re choosing.

  • 44 mm stainless steel case, 20 mm lug width
  • E168 Eco-Drive, powered by light
  • 200 m water resistance (scuba diving)
  • Anti-reflective mineral crystal, luminous hands and markers
