Measure, then decide

Record daily spend, impressions, ad clicks, landing sessions, outbound Amazon clicks, shipped qualifying orders and actual commissions. Use the dedicated paid tag to separate paid results from organic. Do not treat Amazon checkout as observable on our website or upload outbound clicks as purchase conversions.

  • Pause immediately for broken links, irrelevant search traffic or attribution failures.
  • At $15 spent or 30 landing sessions, inspect the funnel. Fewer than 20% clicking through to Amazon is a diagnostic trigger to improve message/offer fit, not a universal industry benchmark. Small samples are noisy.
  • At the fixed end date, stop. Allow Amazon reporting and shipping to catch up before evaluating. Do not renew the campaign automatically.
  • Only scale after actual paid-attributed commission per visitor exceeds cost per visitor with a margin for returns. Ten clicks or ten orders alone do not establish profitability.

Illustration, not forecast: If an order earns $6, 5% of Amazon clickers buy and 30% of landing visitors click through, expected commission per landing visitor is $6 × 0.05 × 0.30 = $0.09. A $0.25 paid click would lose money under those assumptions. Use your actual commission and conversion data, not these example values.
